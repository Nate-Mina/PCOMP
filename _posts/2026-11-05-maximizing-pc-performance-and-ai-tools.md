Hardware upgrades are often the first thing people think of when systems slow down, but intelligent optimization can frequently unlock up to 30-50% more usable throughput from your existing setup.

### 1. Thermal Throttling Mitigation
Modern CPUs and GPUs aggressively throttle clock speeds when junction temperatures cross threshold ceilings. Re-pasting, cleaning heat dissipation fins, and setting proper intake/exhaust static pressure profiles deliver immediate latency and frame-time stabilization.

### 2. Driver Clean-Installs
Residual driver keys from previous display drivers or chipset components often cause micro-stutters. Clean DDU cycles and optimized manufacturer firmware can resolve lingering interrupt issues.

### 3. AI Workload Offloading
With local AI models and Neural Processing Units (NPUs) entering everyday workflows, configuring direct GPU compute paths (DirectML, TensorRT, CUDA) ensures that background intelligence tasks never cannibalize your active gaming or creative rendering threads.
