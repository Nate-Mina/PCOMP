PuCo's Pro Performance Principles

Is your PC stuttering in game, dropping frames during stream broadcasts, or taking minutes to boot? Most modern Windows machines carry hundreds of background telemetry threads, conflicting drivers, and unnecessary vendor bloatware.

1. Programs You Should Never Install on Your PC

Many advertised "cleaners", free driver updaters, and third-party antivirus suites cause more damage than good by injecting registry hooks, background cryptominers, and persistent surveillance telemetry.

"A clean, debloated Windows install with proper manufacturer drivers will outperform any system running 'booster' software by up to 25% in 1% low frame rates."

2. What Pure Computers Does During an Optimization:

  • DPC & Interrupt Latency Tuning: Configures interrupt steering and audio drivers to prevent mouse stuttering and micro-freezes.
  • Background Telemetry Removal: Disables telemetry tasks, Xbox background overlays (unless actively used), and unnecessary autostart agents.
  • Thermal Repasting & Fan Curve Profiling: Applies high-conductivity thermal interface material (TIM) and tunes BIOS PWM curves for whisper-quiet idle and sustained turbo clocks.
  • Memory Profiling: Enables EXPO/XMP profiles correctly and stress-tests memory controllers to avoid random blue-screen errors (BSOD).
