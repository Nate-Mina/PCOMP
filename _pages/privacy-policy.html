Pure Computers Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

At Pure Computers, we respect your privacy. This policy describes how we collect, protect, and use personal information gathered when you visit our website or use our repair and consulting services.

Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us (such as name, email address, phone number, and system specs) when requesting a consultation, estimate, or technical support.

Client Data & Hardware Privacy

When you entrust your computer to Pure Computers for repair or diagnostic service:

  • We never browse personal photos, documents, or private files.
  • Diagnostics are strictly focused on hardware health, operating system integrity, and resolving reported errors.
  • Customer data is never sold, leased, or distributed to third parties.
