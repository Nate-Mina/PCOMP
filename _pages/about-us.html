What Is Pure Computers All About?

Pure Computers is a dedicated tech business committed to exceeding your expectations in computer customization, repair, and AI-powered performance.

Whether you're a creative professional needing a high-throughput workstation, a business seeking to streamline operations through intelligent automation, or a gamer seeking the ultimate battle station, we build tailor-made solutions that keep you ahead.

Our Core Philosophy

  • Customer-Centric: We listen to your real use cases before recommending a single part.
  • Technical Excellence: Deep knowledge of silicon architectures, motherboard VRMs, and kernel scheduling.
  • Honest Transparency: Zero diagnostic markups, detailed quotes, and honest advice.
  • Community Commitment: Proudly serving Rochester, NY locally and users nationwide via remote tech sessions.
Founder & Revitalization

A Decade of Passion for Hardware

Pure Computers was founded over a decade ago with a simple mission: to make technology accessible, dependable, and empowering. Over the years, software has grown more bloated and systems more convoluted. We cut through the noise by building computers that do exactly what you want, quickly and quietly.

"Pure Uncomplicates Work, by making your tech as simple as it can be but not any simpler!"

Services We Specialize In

  • Hand-Tuned Gaming Rigs: Built with premium thermal paste, balanced component pairings, and stress-tested overclocks.
  • Component-Level Diagnostics: Replacing bad power stages, swollen capacitors, or failed NVMe controllers rather than telling you to throw the machine away.
  • Local AI Acceleration: Workstations tuned for stable diffusion, machine learning models, and local LLMs.
