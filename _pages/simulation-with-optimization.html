Simulation Rig Architecture & Optimization

Simulation workloads demand extreme multi-core throughput, sustained memory bandwidth, and uncompromised thermal headroom. Whether you run telemetry for iRacing, Assetto Corsa, Microsoft Flight Simulator, ANSYS, or SolidWorks simulations, Pure Computers engineers systems with zero micro-stutters.

Our Engineering Standards

  • Sustained Clock Frequencies: We tune VRM power limits, custom BIOS LLC (Load-Line Calibration), and liquid cooling configurations to hold maximum boost clocks under continuous 100% compute loads.
  • Multi-Display & VR Integration: Driver profiling for triple 4K 144Hz setups, ultra-wide curved panels, and VR headsets with latency compensation.
  • Rigid Peripheral Management: Dedicated USB controllers and power delivery to prevent disconnects on direct-drive force-feedback wheels, pedals, and throttles.
